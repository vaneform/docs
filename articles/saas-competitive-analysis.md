# SaaS competitive analysis with traffic and search evidence

Originally published at <https://vaneform.com/guides/saas-competitive-analysis> on 2026-10-04 by Kai · 中文版: <https://vaneform.com/zh/guides/saas-competitive-analysis>

## Summary

A SaaS competitive analysis should change a decision. This guide picks peers that illuminate the same decision (an enterprise suite as context, a focused tool as the closer teacher), compares the task a customer must complete from promise to first usable outcome, and records pricing with its date and the unit that matters to the buyer.

Traffic explains distribution: compare full-site visits on the same period and scope with Vaneform's bulk view, then inspect the receiving pages and ranking terms in Site keywords. Different measurement kinds should not be averaged into one score. A fictional worked decision shows why the smaller product may be the better one to learn from. A 3:48 video walks through a Penpot vs Sketch comparison. The guide ends with a brief that is focused enough to revisit and useful at the next planning meeting. It suggests inviting someone to name the strongest alternative explanation, and updating the brief when pricing units, first-use journeys or integrations change.

## Key points and data

- Video example: penpot.app at about 585.76K estimated visits in September 2026, +11.19% month over month, search 45.1% of traffic.
- Penpot's top regions in the video: United States 18.7%, India 8.5%, China 7.4%.
- Don't let the largest traffic figure pick the shortlist.
- Capture pricing with date and buyer unit (seats, projects, usage, feature gate).
- End with the choice, strongest evidence, key open question and next experiment.
