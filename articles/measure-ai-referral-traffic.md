# How to measure AI referral traffic and GEO outcomes

Originally published at <https://vaneform.com/guides/measure-ai-referral-traffic> on 2026-10-08 by Kai · 中文版: <https://vaneform.com/zh/guides/measure-ai-referral-traffic>

## Summary

An AI answer can cite your article without sending a visitor, and a visitor can arrive without doing what you hoped. This guide separates four things that GEO (generative engine optimization) reporting often merges: AI citations, search impressions, referred visits and conversions, and builds a page-level workflow around evidence you can actually observe.

It walks through setting up the arrival report in GA4 (Traffic acquisition, Landing page with Session source / medium, and session key event rate), summarizes what Google's generative AI performance views and Bing's AI Performance report currently show, and connects improvements to Google's guidance on AI features: crawlable pages, useful text, internal discovery and structured data that matches the page. A teaching example shows that useful visits can grow more slowly than total sessions, and the guide warns that small pages produce dramatic percentages from very few observations. The review ends with one next step: expand the example, make the asset easier to reach, repair measurement or keep observing.

## Key points and data

- Teaching example: sessions grow 50% while sessions with a report start grow 12.5%, so the rate falls from 10% to 7.5%.
- Citations, impressions, referred sessions and conversions are separate measures.
- GA4 path: Reports → Acquisition → Traffic acquisition; pair Landing page with Session source / medium.
- Going from two useful sessions to four is +100% but rests on two observations.
- A zero in one citation dashboard needs its coverage label.
