# Backlink resources: how to evaluate directories and promotion sites

Originally published at <https://vaneform.com/guides/backlink-resources> on 2026-10-04 by Kai · 中文版: <https://vaneform.com/zh/guides/backlink-resources>

## Summary

A backlink resource is worth pursuing when a reader has a credible reason to follow its link to your product. This guide distinguishes placements that do different work (a launch directory helps buyers discover and compare, an integration reference serves someone already using another system) and pairs each with the right landing page.

It recommends giving other authors something worth citing, such as an original dataset, a worked calculation, a maintained glossary or a template, and shows a short, specific outreach note. Community answers should help the person who asked, and paid placements should be judged on their disclosed terms and qualified as sponsored. For tracking, it suggests a resource list with the expected reader, submission URL, required asset, cost, review conditions and last-checked date, plus explicit states from "researching" to "published". Vaneform's backlink resource board can supply current candidates. After publication, inspect the live page and follow its link to confirm it works and points where you expect, and keep a record of what actually happened.

## Key points and data

- Match each placement type to its own landing page.
- Strongest outreach: point to a specific missing piece in an existing article.
- Qualify paid links, e.g. rel="sponsored".
- Track reader, URL, asset, cost, conditions and last-checked date per resource.
- Recheck accepted listings after meaningful product changes.
