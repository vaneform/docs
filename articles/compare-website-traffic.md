# How to compare website traffic across competitors

Originally published at <https://vaneform.com/guides/compare-website-traffic> on 2026-10-08 by Kai · 中文版: <https://vaneform.com/zh/guides/compare-website-traffic>

## Summary

Comparing competitor traffic is only meaningful when the numbers share a period and a scope. This guide uses two privacy-friendly analytics products, Umami and Simple Analytics, with real June–August 2026 estimates from successful Vaneform API reads on 9 October 2026, and a downloadable CSV so you can redo the math.

It separates three different questions that often get blended into one: scale (who is bigger), added visits (who gained the most in absolute terms) and relative growth (who grew fastest from their own baseline). Indexing each site's June value to 100 makes the relative paths easy to compare, and the latest month can tell yet another story. The guide then shows how channel clues, such as each site's search share, point to a specific receiving page or search result worth inspecting, and ends with a bounded research brief rather than a vague "they are winning" conclusion. A worksheet section shows how to recompute the ratios yourself, including each site's share of the pair's combined August visits.

## Key points and data

- In August 2026 Umami had about 4.48× Simple Analytics' estimated visits (481,833 vs 107,563).
- Umami: 410,097 → 397,053 → 481,833 (June–August); Simple Analytics: 79,593 → 113,086 → 107,563.
- Across June–August Umami added 71,736 estimated visits (+17.49%).
- August public reports showed rounded search shares of 10.0% (Umami) and 9.0% (Simple Analytics).
- Scale, added visits and growth rate answer different questions; sort and label them separately.
