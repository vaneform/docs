# Keyword research methods: a repeatable workflow

Originally published at <https://vaneform.com/guides/keyword-research-methods> on 2026-10-04 by Kai · 中文版: <https://vaneform.com/zh/guides/keyword-research-methods>

## Summary

The right keyword research method depends on the evidence you already have. This guide describes three starting points. With customers but little search data, use the words people use while finishing a task. When competitors already serve the category, inspect their ranking terms beside the landing pages. When your site already has impressions, start from Search Console query groups by page.

It shows why an aggregate click-through rate can drop just because the query mix changed, even when the original group did not weaken, and walks one incomplete-month question through three possible research decisions. Each decision is kept in a record with the exact query, evidence source, intended reader, proposed URL and contribution. When methods disagree, the guide picks the evidence closest to the task and keeps the disagreement in the note for later. It also recommends using complete date windows and checking seasonality where it could matter, so that a comparison between two periods is not distorted by partial months.

## Key points and data

- Three methods: customer language, competitor terms, own Search Console data.
- Example: a new query group can pull combined CTR to about 1.47% while the original group stays at 10%.
- Record query, evidence source, reader, URL and contribution for each decision.
- Keep the first research round small enough to publish from.
- Revisit when missing evidence arrives or the product changes.
