# How to evaluate SaaS directories before submitting

Originally published at <https://vaneform.com/guides/evaluate-saas-directories> on 2026-10-08 by Kai · 中文版: <https://vaneform.com/zh/guides/evaluate-saas-directories>

## Summary

A directory with a big audience can still bury your listing in the wrong category. This guide gives a simple skip / investigate / submit decision for SaaS directories based on audience fit, listing quality, a working discovery path and the effort required. It assumes no ranking benefit and judges a placement on the relevant visitors it sends.

An invented comparison of two placements shows why referral volume alone misleads: the one with more sessions can produce far fewer activations. The guide quotes Google's link-spam definition and explains how paid or sponsored placements should be qualified, how to prepare one accurate product profile and adapt it per directory, and how to tag each listing with campaign parameters so referrals show up in GA4. It also covers what an uneventful first month means and why your own time belongs in the cost. A sensible outcome can be keeping an accurate listing without further effort, fixing one mismatch, or testing another relevant category.

## Key points and data

- Decision rule: skip when the category is irrelevant or links are broken; investigate unclear fees; submit small batches when the discovery path works.
- Example: a directory sending 300 sessions with 3 activations converts at 1%, against 10% elsewhere.
- Google defines link spam as links created primarily to manipulate rankings (checked 2026-10-16).
- Sample tag: ?utm_source=directory_a&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=launch.
- Count preparation and maintenance time per listing.
