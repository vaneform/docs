# How to find competitor keywords from a website

Originally published at <https://vaneform.com/guides/find-competitor-keywords> on 2026-10-04 by Kai · 中文版: <https://vaneform.com/zh/guides/find-competitor-keywords>

## Summary

A competitor keyword list is useful when it reveals a customer task you can serve. This guide starts by choosing the right competitors for the question (a product competitor and a search competitor can be different sites) and suggests two direct peers plus one site that keeps appearing for a problem query you care about.

It then reads each keyword beside the page that ranks, using Vaneform's Site keywords view, and warns against manufacturing a "keyword gap": a query absent from your export is absent from that sample, not proven missing from your site. Comparable datasets are needed before counting overlaps, and Search Console is the reference for your own property. A worked invoice-reminder example turns a query into a writer-ready brief, and the guide describes a miniature research file per publishing candidate: the phrase, the competitor URL, the task, your contribution and an alternative decision. For a batch of candidates, keep these files separate and pick the ones with the clearest task.

## Key points and data

- Pick two direct peers and one recurring search competitor.
- Read representative rows manually; review excluded brand names when integrations matter.
- Absence in a compared sample is not proof of a gap.
- Example brief: three editable overdue-invoice reminder emails with a completed example.
- Stop research once the first candidate has an audience, an output, a URL and material.
