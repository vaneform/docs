# How to find a competitor's top pages from keywords

Originally published at <https://vaneform.com/guides/find-competitor-top-pages> on 2026-10-08 by Kai · 中文版: <https://vaneform.com/zh/guides/find-competitor-top-pages>

## Summary

A competitor keyword export can contain hundreds of rows and still not tell you which page to build. This guide groups keywords by landing page instead, separates brand demand from discovery demand, and turns the strongest non-brand page into an original content brief.

A worked (invented) example for a scheduling product shows how much filtering matters. A meeting-template page contributes about 19% of a keyword sample's estimated search visits, but about 57% once brand queries are removed, while its estimated contribution stays the same. The guide explains what "top pages" means in a ranked-keyword sample, why missing URLs and estimates should stay visible, and how to use Vaneform's Site keywords view with a Non-brand filter and the Landing pages tab. It finishes with a brief for a weekly team meeting template and how to validate the page in Search Console after publishing. Before commissioning a series, the guide recommends building one brief to completion and reviewing the resulting draft against it.

## Key points and data

- Example sample: 1,050 estimated search visits in total; the template page contributes 200 (about 19%).
- After excluding brand queries the remaining sample is 350 and the template's share rises to about 57.1%.
- Label every filter: a share without its filter is misleading.
- In Vaneform: Site keywords → Non-brand terms → Landing pages.
- Choose the page that matches a real audience task, not just the biggest number.
