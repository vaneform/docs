## Summary

The best free website traffic checker for a first competitor scan may be a poor fit for deciding which article to write. This guide groups free checkers by the question each one answers, using their public descriptions checked on 8 October 2026: Vaneform and traffic.cv for a quick whole-site view, Ahrefs' free Traffic Checker for search traffic, Statvoo for domain context such as DNS, SSL and WHOIS fields, and Search Console and your own analytics when you own the site.

It explains what "free" needs to cover (public lookup vs free account vs trial vs preview), that website and API access can have separate allowances, and why a whole-site figure and a search-only figure should both keep their labels. A 2:38 video walks through checking figma.com on Vaneform. The suggested trial: test one established domain and one small site you actually need, then write down the task, the tool, the reason and the remaining limitation. Record the time it took to get a usable answer as well; it is part of the cost.

## Key points and data

- Grouped by job: whole-site view, search traffic, domain context, owner data.
- Tool descriptions were checked on 2026-10-08.
- A 50,000-visit whole-site figure and an 8,000 search figure can both be right; keep both labels.
- A missing estimate for a small domain is useful information about coverage.
