# Search intent

Originally published at <https://vaneform.com/guides/search-intent> on 2026-10-04 by Kai · 中文版: <https://vaneform.com/zh/guides/search-intent>

## Summary

Search intent is the job behind a query. This guide shows that similar words can lead to very different page experiences: "website traffic checker" wants a tool, "how accurate are traffic estimates" wants an explanation, and "website traffic API" wants documentation. It suggests briefing each page with one outcome sentence ("after reading or using this page, the visitor can ___").

It treats the results page as evidence of the expected experience for a given market, while noting that results vary by time, location and device. Mixed-intent queries such as "meeting agenda" need a clear first screen, and some deserve separate pages. The guide includes a pre-publication review where you behave as the intended visitor and note the first point where the task stalls, plus common gaps: a checker page with no input box, a comparison with features but no verdict, and a tutorial with no expected result. After publishing, it compares incoming queries with the job the page was assigned.

## Key points and data

- Brief each page with one outcome sentence.
- Inspect representative results for your market as evidence of expected format.
- Mixed intent needs a clear first screen and sometimes separate pages.
- Review drafts by doing the task as the visitor and noting where it stalls.
- When queries match but readers can't finish, fix the experience before the keyword.
